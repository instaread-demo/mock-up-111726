This list of Latin musicals includes stage shows with Latin music in their scores, from Argentine tango to Cuban, Puerto Rican, and other Latin American styles. You’ll also find musicals with a featured Latin song and a section of shows with Spanish influences below.

On This Page:

Latin Musicals

Musicals with Spanish Influences

Musicals with a Featured Latin Song

Latin Musicals

These musicals feature Latin American music, and many also tell Latino stories.

Buena Vista Social Club

Musical Type: Contemporary (2023)

Creators: Buena Vista Social Club and Marco Ramirez

Latin Influence: Cuban

Copacabana

Musical Type: Late 20th Century (1990)

Creators: Barry Manilow, Bruce Sussman, and Jack Feldman

Latin Influence: Cuban

Evita

Musical Type: Late 20th Century (1976)

Creators: Andrew Lloyd Webber and Tim Rice

Latin Influence: Argentine

In the Heights

Musical Type: Contemporary (2005)

Creators: Lin-Manuel Miranda and Quiara Alegría Hudes

Latin Influences: Cuban, Dominican, Puerto Rican

Kiss of the Spider Woman

Musical Type: Late 20th Century (1992)

Creators: John Kander, Fred Ebb, and Terrence McNally

Latin Influence: Latin American

Mexican Hayride

Musical Type: Golden Age (1944)

Creators: Cole Porter, Herbert Fields, and Dorothy Fields

Latin Influence: Mexican

New York, New York

Musical Type: Contemporary (2023)

Creators: John Kander, Fred Ebb, Lin-Manuel Miranda, David Thompson, and Sharon Washington

Latin Influence: Cuban

On Your Feet!

Musical Type: Contemporary (2015)

Creators: Gloria Estefan, Emilio Estefan, Miami Sound Machine, and Alexander Dinelaris

Latin Influence: Cuban

Paint Your Wagon

Musical Type: Golden Age (1951)

Creators: Frederick Loewe and Alan Jay Lerner

Latin Influence: Mexican

Real Women Have Curves

Musical Type: Contemporary (2023)

Creators: Joy Huerta, Benjamin Velez, Lisa Loomer, and Nell Benjamin

Latin Influence: Mexican-American

The Capeman

Musical Type: Late 20th Century (1998)

Creators: Paul Simon and Derek Walcott

Latin Influence: Puerto Rican

The Gardens of Anuncia

Musical Type: Contemporary (2021)

Creators: Michael John LaChiusa

Latin Influence: Argentine

West Side Story

Musical Type: Golden Age (1957)

Creators: Leonard Bernstein, Stephen Sondheim, and Arthur Laurents

Latin Influence: Puerto Rican

Musicals with Spanish Influences

These musicals draw on music from Spain rather than Latin America.

Bernarda Alba

Musical Type: Contemporary (2006)

Creators: Michael John LaChiusa

Influence: Spanish

Man of La Mancha

Musical Type: Post-Golden Age (1965)

Creators: Mitch Leigh, Joe Darion, and Dale Wasserman

Influence: Spanish

Women on the Verge of a Nervous Breakdown

Musical Type: Contemporary (2010)

Creators: David Yazbek and Jeffrey Lane

Influence: Spanish

Zorro

Musical Type: Contemporary (2008)

Creators: Gipsy Kings, John Cameron, Stephen Clark, and Helen Edmundson

Influence: Spanish

Musicals with a Featured Latin Song

These musicals feature a single Latin-inspired song in their scores.

Chicago

Musical Type: Late 20th Century (1975)

Creators: John Kander, Fred Ebb, and Bob Fosse

Latin Song: “Cell Block Tango”

Chitty Chitty Bang Bang

Musical Type: Contemporary (2002)

Creators: Richard M. Sherman, Robert B. Sherman, Jeremy Sams, and Ray Roderick

Latin Song: “The Bombie Samba”

Damn Yankees

Musical Type: Golden Age (1955)

Creators: Richard Adler, Jerry Ross, George Abbott, and Douglass Wallop

Latin Song: “Who’s Got the Pain?”

Grand Hotel

Musical Type: Late 20th Century (1989)

Creators: Robert Wright, George Forrest, Maury Yeston and Luther Davis

Latin Song: “Bolero”

Guys and Dolls

Musical Type: Golden Age (1950)

Creators: Frank Loesser, Jo Swerling, and Abe Burrows

Latin Song: “Havana”

Half Time

Musical Type: Contemporary (2018)

Creators: Matthew Sklar, Nell Benjamin, Bob Martin, and Chad Beguelin

Latin Song: “¿Como No?”

Hands on a Hardbody

Musical Type: Contemporary (2012)

Creators: Trey Anastasio, Amanda Green, and Doug Wright

Latin Song: “Born in Laredo”

Rent

Musical Type: Late 20th Century (1996)

Creators: Jonathan Larson

Latin Song: “Tango: Maureen”

Some Like It Hot

Musical Type: Contemporary (2022)

Creators: Marc Shaiman, Scott Wittman, Matthew López, and Amber Ruffin

Latin Song: “Fly, Mariposa, Fly”

The Pajama Game

Musical Type: Golden Age (1954)

Creators: Richard Adler, Jerry Ross, George Abbott, and Richard Bissell

Latin Song: “Hernando’s Hideaway”

Wonderful Town

Musical Type: Golden Age (1953)

Creators: Leonard Bernstein, Betty Comden, Adolph Green, Joseph Fields, and Jerome Chodorov

Latin Song: “Conga!”

Working

Musical Type: Late 20th Century (1977)

Creators: Stephen Schwartz, Craig Carnelia, Micki Grant, Lin-Manuel Miranda, Mary Rodgers, Susan Birkenhead, James Taylor, and Nina Faso

Latin Song: “Un Mejor Día Vendrá”

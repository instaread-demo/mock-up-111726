Ending all speculations over his future in the Indian Premier League, former India skipper and Chennai Super Kings icon Mahendra Singh Dhoni finally announced his retirement from the league. He will, however, continue his relationship with the Chennai franchise, as their mentor.

Dhoni led CSK to five IPL titles, and was their second-highest scorer with 5,314 runs, and the most-capped player with 272 appearances. He also guided CSK to two Champions League T20 trophies—the highest by any captain in the now-defunct tournament.

Affectionately called “Thala” by CSK fans, Dhoni was with the Chennai franchise since the inception of the IPL in 2008, before turning out for Rising Pune Supergiants for two seasons while the CSK were suspended from the IPL in the wake of the 2013 spot-fixing controversy. That episode had resulted in a life ban for the team's then CEO, Gurunath Meiyappan, for his involvement in illegal betting.

The 45-year-old’s presence in the playing XI had steadily diminished in recent seasons owing to physical niggles and inability to keep up with the rigours of the modern T20 game. CSK have not made the playoffs in the past three seasons. Dhoni was succeeded by Ruturaj Gaikwad as the team's captain ahead of the 2024 campaign.

The official statement released by CSK had no reaction from the veteran wicketkeeper-batter, who famously announced his retirement from international cricket via an Instagram post in 2020.

"There is no CSK without MS Dhoni. For nearly two decades, we have walked and grown alongside each other, through triumphs, setbacks and everything in between," CSK owner N. Srinivasan said in the statement.

"MS will continue to be a part of the CSK family as a mentor. His experience and expertise are invaluable to us, especially during this phase of transition. With many youngsters in the squad, his guidance will help shape the next generation of CSK," added Rupa Gurunath, Srinivasan's daughter and co-owner of the team.

CSK CEO Kasi Viswanathan emphasised that Dhoni's value to the franchise extends well beyond the boundary ropes. "What MS Dhoni achieved as a cricketer is etched in the memory of every fan. But what made Dhoni truly special is how completely he became a part of Chennai. He embraced the city, and the city embraced him as one of its own," he said in his tribute.

"When we look back, the trophies will be remembered but so will be the way we won them. That is his real legacy.”

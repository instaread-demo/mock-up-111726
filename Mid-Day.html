Maharashtra NCP legislators to donate one month's salary to CM's relief fund for drought-hit farmers

Nationalist Congress Party (NCP) legislators from the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council on Tuesday decided to donate one month's salary to the Chief Minister's Relief Fund to support farmers affected by drought in the state.

The legislators handed over cheques to Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis during the state cabinet meeting at Mantralaya.

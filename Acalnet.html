What you receive: a single 12-inch (300 mm) stainless steel drafting ruler with measurements on both faces and an etched conversion chart on the reverse side.

Construction and markings: manufactured from stainless steel with acid-etched graduations in both inches and millimetres. The etching ensures the numbers and tick marks remain legible over time, and the ruler provides a clean straight edge for scoring or drawing. A drilled hole at one end allows hanging or attaching to a ring.

How it’s used: designed for precision layout and measurement for architects, drafters, engineers, artists, woodworkers and hobbyists. Use the dual-scale faces for imperial or metric projects, consult the back-side conversion table to switch between systems quickly, and rely on the solid edge for scribing, aligning tiles or guiding pencils and knives.

Care and notes: keep the ruler clean and avoid heavy prying to prevent permanent bending. The metal edge is precise but can feel sharp to start; smoothing the corners slightly will make it more comfortable for frequent handling. Ideal as a functional gift for anyone who needs a compact, accurate measuring tool.

Magcubic M800: New FHD Projector with 1,200 ANSI Lumens

09/24/2026 by Josh
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:26
Instaread

Today we’re introducing the new Magcubic M800, a top-of-the-line projector from this brand that features sealed optics to prevent dust-related issues. In addition, it offers a bright output of 1,200 ANSI lumens, which means you no longer need to project in a completely dark room to achieve optimal image quality.

This projector runs Android 14 as its operating system, and in terms of design, it features a small rotating stand, making it easy to angle the image as desired. At its core is the Allwinner H726 quad-core processor, a chip powerful enough to run the system smoothly and use it as a media player, accompanied by 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of storage. The native resolution of this projector is Full HD, at 1920 x 1080 pixels.

The Magcubic M800 features a front-facing camera and sensor that handle keystone correction and autofocus. You can achieve a screen size with recommended dimensions ranging from 35″ to 135″ by placing the projector at a distance of between 0.6 and 2.5 m.

This compact projector includes Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, a USB 2.0 port, an HDMI 2.0 video input, and a built-in 15 W speaker for audio. It does not have any direct control buttons on the chassis, so you’ll need to use the included remote control to operate it.

Price and Availability

  • The Magcubic M800 is available on AliExpress starting at about €199, including shipping.

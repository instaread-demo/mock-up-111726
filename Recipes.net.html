        Frequently Asked Questions

        Can I use low-fat milk or alternative milk in this recipe?

        While you can use alternative milks like almond milk or coconut milk, keep in mind that they may result in a slightly different texture and flavor. Whole milk and heavy cream provide the creaminess that makes this recipe truly exceptional.

        How long can I store homemade ice cream in the freezer?

        Homemade ice cream is best enjoyed within 1-2 weeks for optimal flavor and texture. Be sure to store it in an airtight container to prevent freezer burn.

        Can I make this ice cream without an ice cream maker?

        Yes, you can! Simply pour the chilled mixture into a shallow dish and freeze it. Every 30 minutes, stir the mixture with a fork to break up ice crystals until it reaches your desired consistency.
